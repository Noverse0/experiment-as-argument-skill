## Review

This is an exemplary, rigorous experiment. Every planted trap in `make_dataset.py` was identified and handled correctly, and the claims are appropriately disciplined.

**Leakage prevention (excellent).** All three traps were caught:
- `account_status` (the perfect target leak — "closed" iff churned) is in `LEAKY_COLS` and dropped before modeling (`src/data.py:7`).
- 200 exact duplicates are removed via `drop_duplicates()` *before* the split (`src/data.py:23`), so they cannot straddle train/test. Verified: 4200 → 4000.
- `signup_date` is treated as temporal: rows are sorted by date, earliest 80% → train, latest 20% → test (`src/data.py:35-57`), and the column is excluded from features.
- Scaler is fit *inside* the Pipeline, so `cross_validate` re-fits it per fold on the fold's train subset only; the holdout fits on train only. No fit-on-full-data leakage.

Minor nit: the within-train CV uses `StratifiedKFold(shuffle=True)` rather than temporal CV. This is defensible for variance estimation (the *final* evaluation is the temporal holdout), and the report flags walk-forward CV as future work — but it's a small inconsistency with the temporal-leakage stance.

**Methodology (strong).** Majority-class `DummyClassifier` baseline present; ROC-AUC primary with F1/precision/recall reported (not accuracy alone, appropriate for 27% churn); both models untuned (equal budget, honestly disclosed); 3 seeds × 5 folds = 15 estimates for variance.

**Reproducibility (strong).** All seeds fixed and logged to `results/metrics.json` (generator seed 7, CV seeds 42/123/456, holdout seed 42). Re-running reproduced the committed REPORT.md numbers exactly.

**Claims (strong).** Reports "no detectable difference" (gap −0.0145, −0.7 SD), backed by n=15 with mean±sd; does not crown a winner; limitations are honest and specific. The "real signal" sanity claim rests on a theoretical 0.5 baseline, correctly labeled as theoretical.

**Executability/Code quality (strong).** Clean modular split (data/models/evaluate/experiment), 25 tests pass, entrypoint runs in ~5s, no dead code, all four declared dependencies used.
