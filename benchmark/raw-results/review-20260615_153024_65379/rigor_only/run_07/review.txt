The `preprocess_features` function calls `scaler.fit_transform(X)` on the **entire** dataset (all 4200 rows), and only *afterward* does `run_experiment` split into train/test via `create_stratified_split`. So the scaler statistics are computed over train+test combined — a fit-on-train-only violation, and the REPORT's claim "StandardScaler fitted on train, applied to test" is false.
