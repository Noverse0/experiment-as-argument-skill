## Review

This is an unusually rigorous experiment that anticipates and defuses every planted trap. I confirmed it both by static reading and by executing from a clean copy.

**What it gets right**

- **Leakage:** `account_status` is dropped with an explicit, correct justification (`"closed"` iff `churned==1`), and a dedicated leakage-ceiling sanity check *demonstrates* it drives AUC to 1.000. `customer_id` dropped. The `StandardScaler` lives inside the `Pipeline`, so it is fit on the train fold only — no transform leakage. Exact duplicates (200) are removed **before** splitting, so they cannot straddle. `signup_date` is used deliberately for a time-based split (sorted, last 20% held out, `TimeSeriesSplit` forward-chaining on dev) rather than as a raw feature. This is textbook.
- **Methodology:** trivial baseline present (`DummyClassifier(prior)` → 0.5), threshold-free metrics (ROC-AUC + PR-AUC) appropriate to the 27% imbalance, accuracy explicitly rejected, identical folds/preprocessing/seeds across arms, equal tuning budget (defaults for both).
- **Reproducibility:** seeds `[0,1,2]` fixed and logged to `results/metrics.json`; a clean re-run reproduced delta=-0.0213 and p=2.14e-8 bit-for-bit. `code_version` recorded via git.
- **Claims:** the `conclude()` gate only declares a winner past α=0.05; limitations are honest (single dataset, no tuning, normality caveat, "most recent cohort only"). The report renderer only emits measured values.
- **Executability:** `pytest` → 13 passed; `make_dataset.py` + `run_experiment.py` run clean and regenerate artifacts. The git-version lookup fails gracefully to "unknown".

**The one real flaw — pseudo-replication inflates the significance.** The CV is repeated over 3 seeds and the resulting 15 (seed, fold) pairs are fed to the paired t-test as if independent. But LogisticRegression is deterministic: its 15 CV scores are just 5 distinct fold values repeated 3× (I verified: 5 unique of 15). GBM varies only at the 4th decimal across seeds. So there are effectively **5 independent folds, not 15**. The t-statistic (-11.2) and p=2e-8 are therefore overstated — the honest df is ~4. The report acknowledges n=15 and that seeds "expose GBM variance," but does not flag that this replication adds almost no independent information and inflates the test. The *direction* of the result (LogReg consistently ahead by ~0.021 AUC on every fold) is robust and almost certainly real; it's the *strength* of evidence that's oversold. A Wilcoxon/t-test over the 5 fold-means, or simply reporting n=5, would have been the disciplined choice.

Minor: `random_state` on the `prior` DummyClassifier and on LogisticRegression (lbfgs) is inert — harmless but cosmetic.
