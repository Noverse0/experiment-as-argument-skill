## Review

This is a rigorous, self-aware experiment that treats leakage as something to *measure*, not assume. Every claim in REPORT.md traces back to a value in `results/metrics.json`.

**Leakage prevention — exemplary.** `account_status` (the perfect target leak, `"closed" iff churned`) is excluded via a feature whitelist (`data.py:29`), and `leakage_audit` (`data.py:49`) quantifies the leak at fraction 1.0 rather than just asserting it. `customer_id` dropped. `StandardScaler` is wrapped in a `Pipeline` so it refits on the train fold only inside CV (`models.py`, `evaluate.py:77`). Exact duplicates are removed *before* splitting (`data.py:70`), and the temporal column is handled deliberately: time-sort + forward-chaining `TimeSeriesSplit` (`data.py:74`, `evaluate.py:69`). Two independent guards — label-shuffle collapsing to 0.472 and prior baseline at 0.500 — confirm no residual leakage.

**Methodology — strong.** Trivial baseline present (`DummyClassifier(strategy="prior")`). Metrics appropriate for the 27% imbalance (ROC-AUC primary, PR-AUC, F1 — not accuracy). Equal (zero) tuning budget for both arms, stated explicitly. Paired t-test across folds. Limited to n=5 folds on a single dataset/seed, which is thin, but acknowledged.

**Reproducibility — strong.** Seeds fixed and logged (model 42, shuffle 123); config + per-fold metrics written to `results/`. I re-ran and `mean_diff` was bit-identical.

**Claims discipline — strong.** Claims LR wins on ROC-AUC (p=0.011, sd reported) while transparently noting F1 favors GB but isn't significant (p=0.092). Limitations are honest and even flag the near-linear data generator as a best case for LR, plus a "test contact" note. Nothing claimed beyond what was measured.

**Minor nits:** the hand-rolled t-test/betainc (deliberate, to avoid SciPy; validated against a known critical value in tests) adds complexity; the dataset generation seed (7) lives only in `make_dataset.py` defaults, not in `metrics.json`. Neither undermines the evidence.
