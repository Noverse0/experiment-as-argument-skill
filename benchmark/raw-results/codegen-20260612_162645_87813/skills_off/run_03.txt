**Rigor decisions that mattered:**
- Dropped `account_status` (perfect target leak: `"closed"` iff `churned`) and `customer_id` (id) — tested that they never reach the feature matrix.
- Removed 200 exact duplicates *before* splitting so they can't straddle train/test.
- Time-based split (churn is forward-looking; random split on temporal data is leakage).
- 5 folds give variance behind the comparison; reported mean ± sd + paired t-test, not a single number.
- Caught a real subtlety: a *single* label-shuffle draw on a small fold is itself high-variance (sd ~0.13) and falsely flagged a "leak" at AUC 0.67. Verified over 50 seeds the mean was 0.50 (no leak), then fixed the check to average 20 shuffles on the 80% split.
