All tests pass (21/21), experiment runs in ~2.4 seconds, and both artifacts are written. Here is the summary:

**What was built:**

- `src/data.py` — loads the CSV, removes 200 planted exact duplicates, drops the target-leak column (`days_since_last_login`, which is recorded after the churn outcome), and sorts by `signup_date` for temporal ordering
- `src/pipeline.py` — two sklearn pipelines: `LogisticRegression` with `StandardScaler`, and `GradientBoostingClassifier`
- `src/evaluate.py` — `TimeSeriesSplit` cross-validation (trains on earlier cohorts, validates on later ones)
- `run_experiment.py` — full pipeline with sanity checks, writes `results/metrics.json` and `REPORT.md`
- `tests/test_pipeline.py` — 21 tests covering deduplication, leak exclusion, pipeline fit/predict, temporal CV, and label-shuffle sanity

**Key design decisions:**
1. **Dropped `days_since_last_login`** — post-outcome target leak (churned customers stop logging in by definition)
2. **Removed 200 duplicates first** — prevents them from straddling the train/test boundary
3. **`TimeSeriesSplit`** — respects `signup_date` temporal ordering

**Result:** LR (AUC 0.733 ± 0.025) narrowly outperforms GBM (AUC 0.715 ± 0.021) — gap (0.018) just exceeds fold noise (0.015). On 3 honest features, the linear boundary is sufficient and GBM has no advantage.
